About me

Design in the blood, literally.

Designer in Stockholm. Brand, ads, web and UX/UI, and lately a lot of AI.

My dad ran ad agencies, so design has been around me my whole life. As a kid I was hooked on how an idea could turn into something you could actually see.

I studied Graphic Design & Communication at Linköping University, spent a few years at agencies, and today I work as a graphic designer at SoScale Media.

I like being part of the whole thing, from planning and strategy to the last delivery. But I've gone deepest on brand, ads, web design and development, and UX/UI. I'll happily geek out on typography, untangle messy content, build smart components in Figma or hunt for the color palette that makes a brand feel right.

I'm always up for learning a new tool or a new way of thinking if it solves the problem better. Especially now, with everything happening in AI.
