01

Voice

The rule

The evidence brags.
The voice stays calm.

Adjectives come out of the copy and go into the work. Numbers and process carry the confidence, which frees the writing to be plain, curious and generous.

Yes

“50+ pages, a new identity, four people, WordPress. Here's how we thought about it.”

Specific. Credits the team. Lets the reader draw the conclusion.

No

“Award-winning creative visionary delivering best-in-class digital experiences.”

Claims the conclusion instead of earning it.

Accent candidates

Cobalt reads as precision and systems, and holds real tension against warm paper — the recommendation. Signal is energy and advertising. Forest is calm and premium. Pick one in the bar and live with it for a minute.

Rule

Accent never carries meaning on its own. Every state it marks also changes weight, position or wording, so the page still works without colour.
