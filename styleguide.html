02

Typography

Two jobs, two sizes, nothing in between: a headline too big for its frame, and copy small enough to make it look even bigger. The gap in scale is the design.

Display

Bricolage Grotesque

ExtraBold, optical size 12, width 82 for the giants — one word per section, sentence case, sized to a share of the frame and run off one edge. Used for nothing else: every subhead is Satoshi. Set at the smallest optical size on purpose — that is where the face cuts its deepest ink traps. Free, variable, Google Fonts.

Body & compact

Satoshi

Body, compact copy and every subhead (Medium). Quiet next to the giants, which is the point. The signature word is Instrument Serif italic, on a yellow highlighter.

Meta

JetBrains Mono

Technical readouts inside the graphics only — node labels, counters. Labels are no longer set in it: they are small Satoshi on the page, the naming one marked with a small dot of sun.

Colour

Read off one photograph: a white poster in a dark room, one shaft of sun across it. Ink, paper and yellow. No brown, no amber — one colour, and it is the sun.

Warm grey ramp — borders, meta, disabled
One accent, two exposures

The accent is the sun and only the sun. On espresso it is text. On paper yellow text would be 1.4:1, so there it is only ever a surface — a highlighter stroke under the signature word, the fill every button wipes to, rules and dots. Accent text on paper (labels, numbers) is plain ink. Two tokens keep it honest: --c-accent for fills, --c-accent-text for anything you read.

Rule

Accent never carries meaning on its own. Every state it marks also changes weight, position or wording, so the page still works without colour.
