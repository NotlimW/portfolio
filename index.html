Process

Six steps, one thread.

Same order every time. Follow the line and you'll see the last step writes the brief for the next one.

01Frame

Find the real problem.

Before any pixels, I question the brief, talk to the people involved and go through the data and the competition. What comes out is one clear problem and the number we'll judge the work by.

Output Problem statement · Success metric

02Define

Choose the angle.

Audience, insight, positioning and limits, boiled down to a one-page brief everyone agrees on. That way later discussions are about the work, not about what we're trying to do.

Output Creative brief · Insight

03Explore

Go wide, then narrow.

A few different directions, sketched fast and kept cheap. We put them side by side, check them against the brief and kill most of them on purpose.

Output Territories · One direction

04Test

Prove it early.

Prototypes and variants go in front of real people and real numbers before anything gets polished. Whatever doesn't hold up gets changed now, while it's still cheap.

Output Prototypes · Test results

05Build

Made to last.

Full craft, built as a system with components, templates and docs, so the team can keep going long after I've handed it over.

Output Design system · Handover

06Prove

Measure, learn, repeat.

It ships, then we measure it against the goal from step one. Whatever we learn becomes the brief for the next round.

Output Results · Next brief
