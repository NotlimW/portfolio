Process

Six steps, one thread.

The same order every time — follow the line. The last step writes the brief for the next.

10

01Frame

Find the real problem.

Before any pixels: the brief questioned, stakeholders interviewed, data and competitors read. Out of it comes one problem statement — and the number we'll judge the work by.

Output Problem statement · Success metric

02Define

Choose the angle.

Audience, insight, positioning and constraints, narrowed into a one-page brief everyone has signed off — so later debates are about the work, not the goal.

Output Creative brief · Insight

03Explore

Go wide, then narrow.

Several territories, sketched fast and kept cheap. Put side by side, judged against the brief, and most of them killed on purpose.

Output Territories · One direction

04Test

Prove it early.

Prototypes and variants in front of real people and real numbers before anything is polished. What doesn't hold up changes now, while it's still cheap.

Output Prototypes · Test results

05Build

Made to last.

Full craft, built as a system — components, templates, documentation — so the team can keep making it long after I hand it over.

Output Design system · Handover

06Prove

Measure, learn, repeat.

Shipped, then measured against the goal from step one. What we learn becomes the brief for the next round.

Output Results · Next brief

I'm a graphic designer at SoScale Media, a performance-driven content agency. I make digital advertising that has to earn its place — built on customer research, hypothesis-tested, and rebuilt monthly against what the data actually says.

Alongside the static and motion work, I develop the AI-driven workflows the production runs on. It's the closest thing I've found to doing all three of the things I like at once: designing, systematising, and proving it worked.
