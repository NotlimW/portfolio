Three faces, each with one job. The contrast in size is the design.

Giants

Bricolage Grotesque

ExtraBold 800, optical size 12, width 82. The giants and the wordmark. Sentence case, one word per section, sized to a share of the screen and allowed to crop. At opsz 12 the face cuts deep ink traps into every joint, and set this big those traps become the texture.

The scale · from the tokens
Rules
  • Sentence case everywhere. Headlines too.
  • One sun word per heading, at most one per section.
  • Body text and labels get no letter spacing. Only big type tightens.
  • Reading text stays within 68 characters a line, compact copy within 28.
  • No orphans: the last line always holds at least three words.
  • No all caps, no underlines for emphasis, no bold inside paragraphs.

Imagery

  • Formats: 9:16, 4:5 or 16:9. Case heroes may go 4:3.
  • Corners at radius large (8px), media revealed with a soft clip.
  • Real projects and real people. AI imagery only when the piece is about AI.
  • No stock smiles, no duotones, no heavy filters, no drop shadows on photos.
Motion

Smooth, with a faint bounce.

Things glide in and leave a bit faster than they came. Anything you touch settles with a hint of overshoot.

Press a curve to see it move. Durations below.

  • Reveals use the expo out curve: fast start, long soft landing.
  • Only things under the pointer overshoot (attitude). Scroll reveals never bounce.
  • Exits take about 65% of the entrance time.
  • Motion follows the scroll, it doesn't play on its own in a loop.
  • No wobbles or confetti. Calm and deliberate.
  • Reduced motion turns all of it off and lands on the resting state.

Writing rules
  • No em dashes or en dashes, ever. Use a full stop, a comma or a colon.
  • No "Not X, but Y" setups, tidy pairs of opposites or lists of exactly three for rhythm.
  • No buzzwords: leverage, unlock, elevate, seamless, journey, cutting edge.
  • English on the site. Swedish place names stay Swedish.
  • Numbers as figures: 50+ pages, 4 people.
  • Sentence case, and no orphans: the last line keeps at least three words.
    Accessibility
    • Body text passes WCAG AA on both grounds. Faint text is for whispers only, never for something you need to read.
    • Focus rings are drawn in the deep sun, visible on both grounds.
    • Body text never below 15px. Captions go down to 14px, labels to 12px, and 11px is only for things you glance at.
    • Every image gets alt text that says what is in it, in plain words.
    • Reduced motion is respected everywhere.
